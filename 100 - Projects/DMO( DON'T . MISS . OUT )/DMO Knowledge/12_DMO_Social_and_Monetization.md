# DMO — Social & Monetization

## Public identity now

Present the account as the user personally:

- trenching;
- researching;
- filtering noise;
- tracking early plays.

Do not overstate DMO publicly as a fully autonomous AI caller until reliability is strongly proven.

## Public proof

Use:

- honest call screenshots;
- call MC/timestamp;
- winners and losers;
- tracked results;
- NFA / DYOR;
- no "guaranteed" language.

## Private product

Potential private Telegram value:

- selective early opportunities;
- research/context;
- risk labels;
- honest tracking;
- fewer junk calls.

## What is sold

Sell access to research/filtering/community, not guaranteed returns.

## Engineering/business alignment

A smaller stream of early, useful, transparent calls is more valuable than a noisy stream of every coin.
